# Emotional cinematography bible

This is an authored direction and review contract, not a statement that every visual or device test has passed. The supplied raw WhatsApp writing and 34 source photographs have priority over this interpretation. The frozen 66-fragment archive follows them; `journeyNarrative.ts` remains the authority for the existing 12 chapters and 32 scenes. Do not replace canonical prose, fill a redaction, or turn the builder into the destination.

The source export is a collation, as the 2025-12-30 10:45:53 message explicitly explains. Times below identify source messages, not autobiographical chronology. All unqualified times refer to 2025-12-30. Image numbers are the original filename prefixes, for example `00000282` identifies the lantern above a reflecting mirror. Canonical supplemental fragments 063, 064 and 066 are not present in the supplied text export; retain their shipped prose without inventing a WhatsApp timestamp. Fragment 065 repeats the earlier Hope material.

Prose cues below identify the canonical entry and the surface/trigger for its existing text. They are not replacement narration. The renderer must read the archive through the existing validated content path, keep exact punctuation and redaction boundaries, and preserve the accessible DOM reader. An event may reveal a short exact passage while the reader provides the full witnessed fragment.

## Global language

Black reflective water, damp earth and wood, translucent fabric, powder blue, cream, muted rose and restrained amber come from the photographs and the raw 11:11:11 FEELING list. White cloth expresses vulnerability, not a supernatural enemy. Fire has a precise edge against darkness. Small figures and empty air matter as much as objects. No wolf attack, jumpscare, reward fanfare, neon portal, or universal bloom is justified by this adaptation.

Player input wins over camera assistance. Use small eased FOV changes, stable horizons, composition and sound; never snap, roll, shake, or force a third-person cutscene. Reduced motion removes involuntary camera movement and preserves the event through state, light and sound. Assistance can be disabled independently. Blend through the emotional conditions below; a scene boundary is not permission to switch the whole image instantly.

Water evolves from attraction and reflection to truth, grief and life. Fire evolves from warmth to boundary to ember. Wolf becomes protection and rest. Swan retains tenderness through captivity, grief and freedom. Seer becomes discernment without remaining a person to obey. Lantern changes from a moving external invitation to carried direction to a chosen placed light. The crown is recognised in reflection, never awarded.
